05 — Principles

AI without the hype

Looks great.
But does it work in production?

I fight showcase AI: impressive in the demo, but fragile when it meets real data, users, business rules and systems.

A solution needs a well-defined problem, must integrate with existing systems and must allow quality, cost and failures to be evaluated. In AI applications, this also means verifying answers, limiting permissions and defining when a person must step in.

Technology is the means.

Results are the product.
